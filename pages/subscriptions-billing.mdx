# Manage BuildShip Subscriptions & Billing

BuildShip integrates Stripe to manage payments, you can get all your past invoices by logging into your Stripe Account
(with the same email used in your BuildShip account).

Starter includes a 7-day trial. A card is required to start the trial. Compare Starter, Pro, Business, and Enterprise on
the [BuildShip pricing page](https://buildship.com/pricing).

<Image src={subscription} alt='buildship subscription and billing' width={1200} />

<Steps>
### Access Subscriptions Modal

- Click on the Manage or Upgrade button in the bottom left corner of the BuildShip app.

- This opens the subscriptions modal where you can select your current plan and click "Manage Current Plan".

- You will be redirected to Stripe, where you can manage your subscription.

### Stripe Checkout Page (applicable on upgrade or new subscription)

- On the Stripe Checkout Page, you can fill in your payment details.

- You can also add a coupon code if you have one.

<Image src={checkout} alt='buildship stripe checkout' width={1200} />

</Steps>

## Downloading Invoice and Receipts from Stripe Dashboard

To download your invoice and receipts from your Stripe Dashboard, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your Stripe Dashboard https://dashboard.stripe.com.
2. Navigate to the "Billing" or "Payments" section.
3. Look for the "Invoices" or "Receipts" tab.
4. Click on the desired invoice or receipt that you want to download.
5. On the invoice or receipt page, you will find a download button.
6. Click on the download button to get the invoice or receipt.

That's it! You have successfully downloaded your invoice or receipt from your Stripe Dashboard.

Note: Billing address and user information cannot be changed on already fully paid subscriptions. Any change to a fully
paid invoice or receipt requires a new charge (subscription or purchase).
